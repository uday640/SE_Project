Sample Loan Origination Requirements Source

This fictional example is provided for demonstration only. Replace it with approved, jurisdiction-specific lending policies before using the project for real decisions.

The loan application service shall capture the requested principal amount, repayment term, applicant consent, and supporting documents before an application can be submitted.
The service shall verify the applicant identity using the institution-approved identity provider before underwriting begins.
The underwriting workflow shall record the policy version and source data used for each eligibility decision.
The service must not issue a final decline when required applicant data is missing; it shall route the application to an authorised lending reviewer.
The service shall provide an applicant with the application status and the next action required after each decision stage.
Only authorised lending and compliance roles may view applicant financial documents.
The service shall retain the application decision, reviewer identifier, and relevant timestamps in an auditable record according to the approved retention policy.
The loan origination workflow shall make decision criteria and policy changes reviewable by the product owner, risk owner, and compliance officer before release.
The project team shall define and approve response-time, availability, recovery, and transaction-volume targets before production readiness review.
